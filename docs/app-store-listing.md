## Description (4000)
Frugality Pug is a set of friendly money calculators for planning and daydreaming about your future. Everything stays on your iPhone: no account, no ads, no tracking.

CALCULATORS
• Budget: list your monthly income and expenses line by line. Categories are suggested as you type, and you can add your own.
• Take-Home Pay: see your paycheck after federal tax, Social Security, Medicare and state tax for all 50 states and DC. Monthly, semi-monthly or biweekly.
• Retirement: project your savings with contributions, employer match, raises, returns and inflation, in future and today's dollars. Optionally add an estimated Social Security benefit.
• Net Worth: add up assets and debts by category.
• Rent vs Buy: compare renting and buying year by year, including the cost of the down payment you could have invested.
• Rental Property: check a rental against cash flow, cap rate, cash-on-cash return, debt coverage and the 1% rule, and get a quick verdict.
• Property Snowball: see how fast one rental can fund the next.
• Amortization: find what you'll still owe on a loan at any point, and how much extra payments save.

MADE TO BE EASY
• Your entries are saved automatically.
• Charts and year-by-year tables.
• Press and hold to arrange the home screen your way.

Frugality Pug gives estimates for planning and fun. It is not financial, tax, legal or investment advice. Tax and Social Security figures use 2026 numbers.

## App Review notes
