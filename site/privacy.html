Privacy Policy

Frugality Pug · Effective October 2026

Frugality Pug does not collect any data.

The numbers you enter (salaries, budgets, balances, home prices and so on) are stored only on your iPhone, in the app's own storage. Nothing is sent to us or to anyone else.

There is no account, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. The app makes no network requests and does not connect to your bank or any financial account.

Deleting the app deletes all of its data.
