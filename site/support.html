Quick how-to

Open a calculator: tap its tile on the home screen.

Rearrange the home screen: press and hold a tile, then drag it to a new spot.

Your entries are saved: each calculator remembers what you typed, even after you close the app.

Budget categories: type an expense name like "Car insurance" and a category is suggested. Tap the category to pick a different one, or add your own at the bottom.

Social Security (Retirement): turn on "Include Social Security" and enter your typical yearly earnings, years worked and the age you'll start benefits.

Good to know

Results are estimates for planning and fun, not financial, tax, legal or investment advice. Each screen notes what it leaves out.

Tax and Social Security figures use 2026 numbers. Local taxes and state payroll taxes (such as California SDI) aren't included.

Your numbers are stored only on your iPhone. Deleting the app deletes them.
